How it works, step by step

Five steps from her voice to a spoken answer. One FastAPI backend sits between the browser and every other service.

  1. 1

    She speaks or types

    She opens a link on her phone, taps her language and answers a few easy questions. Typing works too.

    Next.js web app

  2. 2

    Voice becomes text

    Speech is converted to text in Hindi, with Gujarati next. She sees what was heard and confirms it.

    BhashiniSarvam backup

  3. 3

    Answers become a profile

    The AI reads her answers and fills a short profile: business, need, place and group membership.

    Gemini Flash

  4. 4

    Rules find the matches

    The backend compares her profile with the verified database and returns the best three. The AI never decides who is eligible.

    FastAPIVerified JSON

  5. 5

    She hears the result

    A short plain-language explanation is read aloud, shown as three cards, and packed into a PDF.

    GeminiText to speechPDF

The rule behind it: the AI explains, the rules decide. Eligibility comes from checked data with a source link, so a wrong guess cannot reach her.

Tech stack, and why we chose each part

Everything below has a free tier or free developer access, so the prototype costs nothing to run.

PartOur choiceWhyOther options
Interface Next.js web app (PWA) on Vercel Opens from a link, installs to the home screen and stays light on basic phones. Telegram or WhatsApp bot, Flutter app
Backend FastAPI (Python) on Render Quick to build, and Python keeps the AI and data work simple. Node and Express, Flask

Application pack HTML to PDF Simple to build, easy to share and print. Word export

Free-tier AI services may use inputs to improve their models, so the prototype runs on sample data only and never on real users’ details.

What makes it different: the Voice-to-Application Pack

Most tools stop at information. We turn the conversation into something she can carry to the bank or service centre: a pre-filled business profile, a document checklist and spoken next steps.

In our research we found no existing tool that does this.

Data roadmap: how the database grows

We start with a small, hand-checked list and replace it in stages, so answers stay correct as the product grows.

  1. Now: the hackathon

    30 to 50 hand-verified entries in a JSON file. Each has eligibility, documents, an official link and a last-verified date.

  2. Next: partners verify

    Move to Supabase and add an admin panel where NGOs, self-help group federations and block offices add and verify local entries.

  3. Later: stays fresh

    A freshness checker flags old entries, official sources feed the database, and “did this work for you?” feedback improves the ranking.

  4. At scale: lower cost

    Self-hosted open Indian-language speech models, cached audio for common answers, and each new state or language added as a data pack, not new code.

Risks we plan for

  • Speech errors. She confirms what was heard, and typing is always available.
  • Outdated data. Every result shows an official link and a last-verified date.
  • Privacy. No login, and audio is not stored.
